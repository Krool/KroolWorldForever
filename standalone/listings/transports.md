## Summary

A pin on the world map at every boat dock and zeppelin tower in WoW Forever, saying where it goes. The routes come from the game's own data, so Forever's new boats are there too.

## Description

- Every boat dock and zeppelin tower gets a pin on the world map. Hover it for where the boat or zeppelin goes.
- The routes are read from the game's own data, so the new ones, such as Stormwind to Darkshore, are there with the classic ones.
- Zone maps show their docks. Continent maps and the world map can show them too, or not.
- Two boats or zeppelins that leave from one place share a pin, which lists both destinations.
- `/kwb` lists the boats and zeppelins of the zone you are in. `/kwb all` lists every dock.
- It does not know when the next one arrives.
- Settings: show on continent maps, and the pin size.

Settings: Esc > Options > AddOns > KW Boats And Zeppelins.
