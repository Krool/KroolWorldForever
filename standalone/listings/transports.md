## Summary

A pin on the world map at every boat dock and zeppelin tower in WoW Forever, saying which town it goes to and whose town that is. The routes come from the game's own data, so Forever's new boats are there too.

## Description

- Every boat dock and zeppelin tower gets a pin on the world map. Hover it for the town the boat or zeppelin goes to, and how long it waits at the dock.
- A town that belongs to one side says (Horde) or (Alliance), in red when it is not yours.
- The routes are read from the game's own data, so the new ones, such as Stormwind to Darkshore, are there with the classic ones.
- Zone maps show their docks. Continent maps and the world map can show them too, or not.
- Two boats or zeppelins that leave from one place share a pin, which lists both destinations.
- Zone maps also show the Deeprun Tram and the portal between Rut'theran Village and Darnassus.
- High Order Skyborne also get a pin at Dalaran's portal to Stormwind, on zone maps. Other characters do not see it.
- `/kwb` lists the boats and zeppelins of the zone you are in, or names the nearest dock. `/kwb <place>` lists what goes to a place. `/kwb go` puts the game's map pin on the nearest dock. `/kwb all` lists every dock.
- It shows every route in the game's data. It cannot tell when the next boat arrives, or whether a route is running yet.
- Settings: show on continent maps, and the pin size.

Settings: Esc > Options > AddOns > KW Boats And Zeppelins.
