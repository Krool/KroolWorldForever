# KW Skill-Up Odds

- **Folder:** KW_SkillUpOdds
- **Main category:** Professions. **More:** Auction & Economy.
- **Relations:** Auctionator, optional.
- **Logo:** `standalone/logos/skillup.png`

## Summary

In the crafting window: the chance each recipe gives a skill point, and a Next Skill Point panel that ranks your recipes by what a point costs.

## Description

- Beside each recipe's arrow in the crafting window: the chance it gives a skill point.
- The chance follows the usual straight line from 100% at yellow to 0% at grey, tuned by every yellow or green craft on your characters. Hover for the numbers and what a point costs.
- A Next Skill Point panel ranks your learned recipes by the gold a point costs on average, and only reorders when a recipe is clearly cheaper.
- Prices come from Auctionator when you have it, and from the vendor prices it has seen.
- `/kwu` shows how your crafts sit against the line, overall and range by range.

Settings: Esc > Options > AddOns > KW Skill-Up Odds.
