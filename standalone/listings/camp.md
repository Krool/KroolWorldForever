# KW Camp Guide

- **Folder:** KW_CampGuide
- **Main category:** Tooltip. **More:** Buffs & Debuffs.
- **Relations:** none.
- **Logo:** `standalone/logos/camp.png`

## Summary

Hover a campfire or a camp object for the buff it gives at your level, and whether a class buff you already have covers it.

## Description

- Hover a campfire or a camp object (the chair, the lodestone and the rest) and its tooltip says the buff it gives at your level.
- It also says whether a class buff you already have covers it. The Lodestone, for one, does not stack with Blessing of Might.
- The campfire explains how its buffs work and lists the objects you have hovered around it.
- The Camp Benefits buff names any bonus in it that one of your class buffs overlaps.
- `/kwc` lists every camp buff at your level and your Camp Benefits time.
- The tooltip lines show while you are at a camp, with Campfire Nearby on you.

Settings: Esc > Options > AddOns > KW Camp Guide.
