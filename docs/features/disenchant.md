# Disenchant Value

On weapon and armor tooltips (green, blue and purple): what the item disenchants into, what that
is worth at Auctionator's prices, and whether disenchanting it pays more than auctioning or
vendoring it. The question it answers: should this BoE green go to a disenchanter, onto the
auction house, or to a vendor?

```
Disenchant                                   27s 75c
Best: disenchant it (auction 20s, vendor 5s)
  Soul Dust 1-2, 75%                          10s each      <- with Shift, or the option
  Greater Astral Essence 1-2, 20%             50s each
  Large Glimmering Shard, 5%                  30s each
```

## Where the numbers come from

- **Tables:** `Features/Disenchant/Data.lua`, generated by `tools/killvalue-data/gen-disenchant.js`
  from the same cmangos classic-db dump as Kill Value (`disenchant_loot_template`,
  `item_template.DisenchantID`). The 1.12 data assigns tables by quality, class (weapon or armor)
  and item level bands; 6,372 of its 7,994 weapons and armor follow their band. 1,346 cannot be
  disenchanted (rings, necklaces and wands among them) and 276 armor pieces use a weapon table.
  Every one of the 7,994 is stored with its own table, so classic items stay exact even if Forever
  reports other item levels. Items not in the 1.12 data (Forever's new items have new ids, such as
  273636 in Auctionator's cache) use the bands.
- **Data fix:** entry 50 (blue, item level 69+) lists only the 0.5% Nexus Crystal; the generator
  gives it entry 49's rows (Large Brilliant Shard 99.5%).
- **Forever:** guides (2026-11) show the same dust, essence and shard tiers by level, armor mostly
  dust and weapons mostly essence, and report no Enchanting skill gate. Not verified in game.
- **Value:** sum over the table of chance x average count x Auctionator price. A material at 10%
  or more without a price leaves the value unknown ("no price for Soul Dust"); rarer ones (shards
  at 5%) count as 0.
- **Best:** the highest of disenchant, the item's own auction price (by link, so random suffixes
  and item levels are told apart) and its vendor price, with the 5% auction cut taken off the
  first two. Ties go to the less work (vendor, then auction). A disenchanter's tip is not counted.
- **Bound items** (bind on pickup, or a tooltip that says Soulbound or Account Bound) skip the
  auction price, and get the Best line only when you can disenchant yourself
  (`C_SpellBook.ContainsAnyDisenchantSpell`).

## Auctionator's own disenchant line

Auctionator 341 has a Disenchant tooltip line, but its TOC loads `Source_Mainline` on Forever
(`[AllowLoadGameType mainline]` matches Forever's game type; `classic` does not), and the Mainline
tables start at Warlords of Draenor. Its Classic tables (`Source_Classic/Enchant`) ship in the same
download but do not load. So items of classic levels get no line from it, and none of ours
duplicates it.

## Settings and commands

Show On Item Tooltips, Say What Pays Best, Always List The Materials (else hold Shift). `/kwd`
prints every material's price (which still need an auction scan); `/kwd options`.

## To check in game

- A green's tooltip shows Disenchant with a value after an Auctionator scan, and the Best line.
- Shift lists the materials. A soulbound item shows no auction compare.
- Auctionator shows no second Disenchant line (if it does, its Forever tables changed).
- Disenchant a few greens and compare with the tables (dust 75% on armor, essence 75% on weapons).
