# What changed, 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-18

Researched 2026-11-18, two weeks after launch. It adds to the other files in this folder, which
stop at about Nov 9; where they disagree, this file is newer. Each item names its source. "Not
confirmed" means no source was found, not that it is false.

## Builds

- **Live is 1.60.1.70959 (2026-11-17), the newest build.** No later build, no Forever PTR product
  and no 1.60.2 on wago.tools or wow-ui-source. The wiki's 1.60.1 pages are stale (they still show
  70790). Sources: wago.tools `/api/builds`, Gethe/wow-ui-source `forever` (commit `3d8abb1`).
- **70938 to 70959:** one new function, `C_Mail.IsCODEnabled()`, and one constant,
  `EncodingDeserializeSizeLimit`. No event or enum changed, nothing removed. FrameXML: the mail
  frame can grey out COD, tutorial and micro menu overrides for Forever, gamepad bag navigation.
  The build table is in `docs/02-addon-platform.md`.
- **Client tables did not change** between 70938 and 70959: SkillLineAbility, SkillLine,
  TaxiPathNode, TaxiPath, TaxiNodes, ItemDisenchantLoot, GlobalStrings (wago.tools CSVs compared).
  Server hotfixes do not show in these exports.

## Blizzard hotfixes (forum thread 2403040)

What matters to the bundle:

- **Auction house (not confirmed live).** The Nov 10 notes had a 1 copper minimum deposit (was
  1 silver) and mail fixes. Blizzard pulled them on Nov 12 and said they would go live with the
  Nov 17 maintenance. The Nov 17 notes do not mention the auction house. Check the deposit in game.
- **Nov 17, camps:** the Cozy Sleeping Bag gives a "Resting" aura while you are in its area (spell
  id not found; Camp Guide could read it). Journeyman Campfire Kit needs Cooking 90 to place, was
  140 (Nov 10).
- **Nov 11, tailoring bags:** Red Mageweave Bag takes 5 bolts (14 slots), Green Silk Pack 5 and
  Black Silk Pack 6 (12 slots), Green and Red Woolen Bag 5 (10 slots). A server hotfix: Reagent
  Uses' counts for these come from the client tables and may be the old ones.
- **Nov 10, vendor prices:** cut for some bind-on-pickup crafted armor; Minor Arcane Elixir sells
  for 5c (was 25c), Iron Grenades for 1s 25c (was 5s). "Recipe: Elixir of Greater Firepower" is
  now "Recipe: Elixir of Holy Power". Arcane Salvager needs a Runed Golden Rod and applies its
  disenchanting bonus to lower-level enchanters. Most quests of level 48 and up also reward gold.
- **Nov 13 and 14, kills:** trivial dungeon enemies give no XP to a party more than 12 levels
  above them. Spawn rates changed in the Badlands, Stranglethorn and Mulgore. Nethergarde Keep
  guards drop no loot.
- **Nov 16 and 17, Blackmaw Hold:** non-elite enemies there dropped full elite loot from Nov 14
  until the Nov 16 hotfix; Blizzard removed gold and suspended accounts. Loot Kill Value learned
  there in those days is skewed.
- **Nov 16, fishing:** the Fishing Rack gives lures to everyone; Bloated Fish drop more often.
  Nov 13: the Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza allows many more winners.
- **Nov 5, transports:** boats and zeppelins were getting lost; Blizzard put temporary portals at
  docks and towers. Whether the portals are gone is not confirmed.
- **No addon policy or API restriction post since launch** was found on the forums (the
  WoWUIDev Discord was not readable).

## Addons we depend on

- **Auctionator 349 (2026-11-18).** The owner's install had 347. `Auctionator.API.v1` is the same
  in 347 and 349 (the `Source/API` folders are identical). What changed behind it, read from the
  349 files:
  - **The price database is per faction from 348.** The key is the ruleset plus the faction
    ("PvE Alliance"). 349 moves the old "PvE" table to the faction of the first character that
    logs in and deletes the old key; the other faction starts empty, so prices read nil there
    until it scans.
  - **Its Classic disenchant tables load on Forever from 348**, so it shows its own Disenchant
    tooltip line on classic-level items, beside Disenchant Value's. Not seen in game yet.
  - 343 separated the RP-PvP house; 347 fixed the black market skewing crafting prices.
- **Questie 12.11.0 and QuestieDB 1.1.3 (2026-11-18).** The owner's install had 12.9.0 and 1.1.2.
  Eleven releases since Nov 7, mostly Forever data. 12.8.0 enabled the Darkmoon Faire on Forever;
  12.11.0 says Forever uses the Era faire schedule (Questie's reading, not Blizzard's). No
  QuestieDB note mentions an API change.
- **BugSack** v12.1.9 is still the latest. BugGrabber's repo could not be reached.
- **BigWigs packager** v2.7.0: still no `_Forever` TOC suffix in its lists, and it maps Forever to
  CurseForge id 88568. **Wago** still lists `"forever":["1.60.1"]`. **WeakAuras** still has no
  Forever TOC.
